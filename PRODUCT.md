# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ML engineers and safety reviewers use AdverTest at a desktop workstation to
measure how a vision model behaves before and after realistic attacks, inspect
evidence, decide whether a failure needs defense training, and verify recovery
against the identical locked benchmark.

## Product Purpose

AdverTest makes the complete robustness loop auditable: clean input and ground
truth, deterministic attack recipe, paired inference, metrics and failures,
defense lineage, re-test, and residual risk. Success is a reproducible report,
not a simulated claim of production safety.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, calm, technically candid.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a decorative neon dashboard, a generic card grid, or an
opaque black-box AI demo. Do not conceal missing SAM2 artefacts or unpaired
comparisons behind optimistic status visuals.

## Design Principles

1. Put source evidence before aggregate scores.
2. Make the next valid action obvious and block invalid actions with reasons.
3. Show pairedness, units, provenance, and limitations where decisions occur.
4. Keep the five-stage investigation sequence readable at a glance.
5. Treat unavailable real-model artefacts as an explicit handoff state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard expectations. Never convey model or
failure status by colour alone. Respect reduced motion and use explicit units
for every numeric value.
